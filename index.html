The Heart of the Adventure

A year in nine chapters,
told for one Mom.

For

Kristen

The planner of the trips,
the keeper of the calendar,
the soft place we all land.

This little book is a year of our lives —
the way only you could make it look.

Chapter One

The Soul of the House

Where every adventure begins

In a home full of sneakers and laughter and noise,
Lives the heart of the family — Mom, and three boys.
With Kamran and Ashton and Ali, her crew,
The year that just passed felt sparkly and new.

But one tiny soul had to travel ahead —
Sweet Kobe, our "wudder butter," our sleepy old head.
In the chill of the winter, he curled up to rest,
Our chocolate dachshund — the bravest, the best.
Though his paws left the floor, he didn't go far —
He watches our travels from a bright, wagging star.

— one —

Chapter Two

Splashes & Sun

May – June 2025 · Gaylord Pacific & Cabo

When the May sun was high, we all hit the road,
Off to Gaylord Pacific where the pool slides flowed.
With splashes and laughter, the boys had a ball —
And Mom made the magic that held it all.

Then June and the breeze and the salt of Los Cabos,
The white sand, the warm sea, the way the sun glows.
With Mom as our compass, our sunshine, our guide,
We chased every wave with the world on our side.

— two —

Chapter Three

The Red & The Gold

Fall 2025 · Niners Season & Thanksgiving in Marin

Then autumn arrived and the jerseys went red —
"Go Niners!" was all that the Chitsaz boys said.
To Arizona we flew, then back to the Bay,
To cheer on our team in the red-and-gold way.

But the truest "huddle" the year had in store
Was Thanksgiving in Marin — the family, and more.
Around a long table, with love in the air,
We gave thanks for the Mom who is always right there.

— three —

Chapter Four

The Winter Carves

Dec 2025 – April 2026 · Tahoe with the Wagners & Mammoth

The mountains were calling, the powder ran deep —
To Tahoe we climbed while the world was asleep.
With Dave, Martha, Mateo — the Wagners in tow,
Our two families painted a streak through the snow.

Through the wind and the frost and the pine-covered heights,
Mom kept us all cozy through cold mountain nights.
From a Tahoe Christmas to Mammoth in spring,
We felt every thrill that the mountains can bring.

— four —

Chapter Five

Red Rocks & Bright Lights

March 2026 · Zion & Las Vegas

For Spring Break we wound through the canyons of red,
With Zion's tall towers right over our head.
We zoomed on our e-bikes and hiked every trail,
And cooled off in salt water when muscles would fail.

Then Vegas! Top Golf! And a show called Mystère —
With Din Tai Fung dumplings — the greatest of fare.
From the dust of the trail to the neon-bright street,
Mom is the reason the journey feels sweet.

— five —

Chapter Six

The Milestone Marches

March 9 – April 28, 2026 · Eight + Thirteen

March ninth — eight candles for Ashton, the wild!
At Universal Studios, our Wizarding child.
Then April brought thirteen for Kamran the tall —
At Magic Mountain we conquered them all.

Thirteen and eight — oh, the boys are so grown,
In the blink of an eye they'll be running their own.
But no matter how big or how brave they may be,
They will always need Mom — and so will I, see?

— six —

Chapter Seven

The Sanctuary

Our place by the sea · Laguna Beach

But sometimes the world is a bit of a rush,
So we look for the blue, and the twilight's soft hush.
To Laguna we drive, to our house by the sea —
The place where we're quiet and happy and free.

Far from the chaos, with salt in our hair,
We breathe in the peace of the cliffside air.
It's our sanctuary home, where we mend and recharge —
Before the next adventure begins, big and large.

— seven —

Chapter Eight

The Anchor

Mother's Day · May 10, 2026

So here's to the planner, the rock, and the light,
Who makes every morning and evening so bright.
From the principal's desk to the Pilates mat,
She wears every cape and she wears every hat.

To the heart of our home and the best Mom we know —
We love you, Kristen, more than these pages can show.
For all that you are, and for all that you do —
Have the happiest Mother's Day, made just for you.

— eight —

Chapter Nine

The Horizon

June 5, 2026 · Curaçao · LionsDive

But hold on — one secret, one trick up our sleeve,
A surprise of the kind only Mom would believe.
On the fifth day of June, we will board a plane,
And leave behind home, the ho-hum, the rain.

To Curaçao's shores where the water is blue,
To celebrate us — and to celebrate you.
With LionsDive waiting and mangroves so green,
The best part of life is the yet-to-be-seen.

— nine —

From your boys

Mom — you are the planner,
the soft place to land,
the cheer in our chaos,
the calm in our hand.

You make every season
a story worth living,
and every small day
feel a little like giving.

Today, tomorrow,
and all of the rest —
we love you, we thank you,
you really are best.

— Ali, Kamran & Ashton
